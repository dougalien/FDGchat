What you will find here

This site brings together three connected parts of the same project: a technical web book, a plain-language companion guide, and an interactive model app. Together, they explore the idea that the universe may be finite, internally measured, and geometrically deformable rather than infinite and governed by one universal observational scale.

The web book develops the model step by step, beginning with questions about infinity, measurement, redshift, and large-scale geometry before moving into supernova observations, BAO structure, the cosmic microwave background, early galaxies, galaxy-scale gravity, voids, compactness, and finite-boundary behavior. The goal is not simply to present equations, but to connect observations that are often treated separately and ask whether they can be understood within a shared finite-response framework.

The companion guide follows the same path in simpler language. It is designed for readers who want to understand the physical ideas and motivations before working through the more technical discussions and calculations.

The interactive app provides a way to explore the model visually, compare observational relationships, and experiment with how different measurement registers behave across scale. Rather than treating the project as a static manuscript, the site is intended to function as a readable and testable exploration of the model as a whole.

The key symbols used throughout the model are summarized in the Measurement Registers page.
